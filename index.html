Kristy Hattaway
Founder • Student • Advocate

This project started with loss.

I've experienced the heartbreak of losing people I love to addiction and depression.

Those experiences changed the way I see healing, connection, and the places where people go when they are struggling.

The Lost & Found Project grew from that place.

I'm studying psychedelic medicine because I want to learn, listen, advocate, and better understand responsible, compassionate, evidence-informed approaches to healing.

I believe there is a larger conversation happening around mental health, connection, consciousness, and new approaches to healing — and I want to be part of it.

I know what it feels like to lose people. Now I'm building toward something rooted in hope.
The Why

What if there is another way?

The questions behind The Lost & Found Project are bigger than one person. They are questions about how we care for one another, how we understand suffering, and how we create more pathways toward connection.

What if being lost doesn't have to be the end of the story?
What if there were more places to feel seen?
What if education created understanding?
What if connection could interrupt isolation?
What if hope had a place to grow?

The Vision

A place to come back to yourself.

"Not an escape from life. A return to it."

One day, I hope to create a retreat center where people can step away from the noise, reconnect with themselves and others, learn, reflect, and explore meaningful pathways toward healing and transformation.
